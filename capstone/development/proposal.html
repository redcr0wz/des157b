Capstone Project Proposal

May 11th, 2026

My capstone project's topic will be focused on the topic on media and entertainment alongside technology. To be more specific, I want to focus on artificial intelligence impacting creative industries and what it may look like in the future. I believe there is a lot to work with regarding speculative design, challenging mental models, data visualization, and utopian/dystopian narratives. I feel like with this topic, a dystopian narrative seems plausible to work with in terms of how AI is impacting creatives. I can also work with how many jobs within the industry have been impacted through data visualization. Overall, I feel like there's a lot of potential surrounding this topic.

In terms of features and overall concepts regarding the project, there are a handful of ideas I have in mind. I would say that the strongest concept I have in mind is telling a dystopian narrative, and how if people continue to dismiss and disregard creative jobs, that people would just continue to take in rehashed ideas again and again. In terms of developing this narrative, I do want to work with libraries that emphasize the visuals I have in mind. I am concerned about my ambitions, the scope, and the timeframe of what I have in mind, but I feel like sitting with this idea would be me make concepts more concrete.

Finally, I believe my audience for this project would be people who believe that AI would genuinely take over creative industries and/or that AI would replace people in creative fields. In the end, as someone who does want to genuinely look into the ethics of artificial intelligence and its involvement with creative industries, I want to provide commentary and a narrative that would impact people under that mental model. I would imagine that these people would be younger adults interested in the advancement in technology and efficiency.
