Project Overview

My capstone project is focused on the influence of artificial intelligence in the realm of entertainment. It is primarily a commentary and showcasing of how artificial intelligence impacts creative fields and how much of an impact "AI slop" takes a toll onto a creator and someone consuming that kind of media.

The layout and design of the project is inspired by short-form content and user interfaces that contain such content. In addition to the layout, it also contains features that allows a user to like and comment from their own device.

The target audience of this project are primarily people who suspect to seeing AI in their feeds. Since AI has been making rounds in all sorts of industries, it's only inevitible for someone average to see AI on television or social media. Additionally, I also wanted to target people who depend on AI as a means to create entertainment. The goal of this project is to showcase the impacts of how much AI has in taking up a good fraction of mainstream media.

Project Research

I initially started my research just looking into the statistics on how AI is used in the entertainment industry, primarily movies and television. After further discussion of my research, I was also looking into how AI impacts other forms of entertainment, such as video games.During this research, I was also working on focusing how I wanted to portray my project further with the support of the teaching team.

Addiitonally, I also looked into platforms like TikTok and Instagram Reels as a source of inspiration in how I wanted the project to look. I was also inspired by how TikTok is oversaturated with AI-generated content, so that connects with my interest in commentating on AI impacting the modern entertainment. I also looked into the user interfaces of generative AI platforms, such as Sora AI. Sora' gallery layout of AI generated video media also inspired me, but I was not able to incorporate it in the end.

Visual Design

As stated in my research, short-form content platforms like TikTok and Instagram reels were the primary source of design inspiration for this project. I drew more inspiration from TikTok and ended up using the font "TikTok Sans" since it was an effective and corporate sans-serif font for the kind of style I wanted to emulate.When working with the common dark-mode look, I felt like red was a good accent color to reflect an ominous alerting feeling, adjacent to alarm lights and "evil" robots represented in media. I also felt like red was a good color to emphasize with amongst the grayscale.

The design approach was actually less involved and intricate than I thought it would be. It actually allowed me to work with the interactive features and fictional branding of the page more. When approaching the logo, I drew inspiration from the movie The Terminator and HAL 9000 from 2001: A Space Odyssey since both are focused on malicious artificial intelliegence. That look was then also translated to the account icons. Overall, I felt like the design approach was straightforward and modern enough to work with.

User Interface

After working with the visual design, I wanted the interactive elements to be the most important part that I focused on during this project.

I first figured out how to lay out "posts" by using Swiper. Using the Swiper library helped mimic the look of scrolling through a post feed while having it technically be an interactable slideshow with code. Then, I focused on laying out a rough draft of the narrative. Then, I worked on having likes and comments be integrated into each post via Back4App. That part of the interactive design was the most time-extensive part of the project. After that, I emphasized the narrative with modals within the JavaScript and animated text via GSAP.

The list of features are as follows:

  • Interactive "slides" meant to mimic video posts
    • one post/video is shown at a time
    • progress bar
    • navigational through scrolling, arrow buttons, arrow keys, and touchscreen
  • Like, comment, and share buttons
    • likes and comments use a backend database to keep track of each post's likes and comments
  • Modals
  • Navigational sidebar (logo refreshes, home sends user back to my portal, the ellipsis are the credits, the other buttons are part of the narrative)
  • Animated text

Revisions

User testing brought a lot of improvements for the project, primarily in terms of the narrative and user experience. Beyond that, testing helped reaffirm that the visual style of the webpage was cohesive and the features were interesting to go through.

In addition, the feedback also had me work with the narrative to make sure that the intentions were there while also having a more flexible interpretation. Alongside, I also improved on making the narrative more engaging to look at especially since it was more long-winded.

Summary

I felt like I learned a lot about working with interactive elements and JavaScript libraries with this project. I also learned a lot about telling narrative through a webpage. It was a very interesting approach in encompassing a narrative within an interactive format.

While working this project, I felt like working with the likes and comments features were the most challenging to implement during the project. I had to consider how lay out my database and how that interacts with the front-end interactable interface for the user.

With that, I am most proud of the interactive elements that I implemented into the project and being able to work a backend database into the project. This project overall was a monumental project when it comes to working with both UI/UX design and web development. I enjoyed applying all th eskills that I have learned for this capstone.
